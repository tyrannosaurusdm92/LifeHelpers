# Hindi

**Family:** Indo-European / Indo-Aryan. **Root and evidence:** Proto-Indo-Aryan ancestry; Vedic Sanskrit is a second-millennium-BCE oral tradition, not a direct written ancestor of modern Hindi.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/hindi/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
