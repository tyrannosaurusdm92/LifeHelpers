# Ancient Egyptian

**Family:** Afroasiatic / Egyptian. **Root and evidence:** Proto-Afroasiatic has highly divergent published dates (roughly 8,000–15,000+ BP); Old Egyptian records are a separate, much later attestation.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/egyptian/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
