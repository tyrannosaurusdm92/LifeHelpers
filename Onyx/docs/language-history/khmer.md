# Khmer

**Family:** Austroasiatic / Khmeric. **Root and evidence:** Proto-Austroasiatic, modeled at roughly 3,000–4,500 BP; dates and homeland remain contested.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/khmer/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
