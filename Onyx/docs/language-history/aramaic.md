# Aramaic

**Family:** Afroasiatic / Northwest Semitic. **Root and evidence:** Early Northwest Semitic / Old Aramaic; inscriptions by the tenth–ninth centuries BCE attest a long, branching tradition.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/aramaic/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
