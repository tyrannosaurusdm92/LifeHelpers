# Georgian (Kartuli)

**Family:** Kartvelian. **Root and evidence:** Proto-Kartvelian; historical estimates range from conservative Bronze-Age scenarios to much older disputed Bayesian proposals.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/georgian/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
