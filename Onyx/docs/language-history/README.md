# Five comparative language histories

Profiles: [Spanish](spanish.md), [Fijian](fijian.md), [Mandarin](mandarin.md), [Yoruba](yoruba.md), [Hebrew](hebrew.md). Expanded, source-linked research is in [research/README.md](research/README.md); the shared code index is `Onyx/js/language-history/research/index.js`.

| Choice | Historical root or early stage | Sibling comparison | Dating caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | Archaic Latin inscriptions, seventh–sixth c. BCE | Romance descendants including Portuguese/Galician and broader Romance relatives | Spanish formed later; inscriptions date Latin writing, not modern Spanish |
| Fijian | Proto-Oceanic c. 3,000–3,300 BP (1950 baseline) calibration | Fijian varieties and Polynesian through later Central Pacific history | Archaeology-informed model, not direct language attestation |
| Mandarin | Sino-Tibetan root estimates c. 5,871–7,983 BP; Old Chinese written evidence in Late Shang | Sinitic branches including Wu, Yue, Min, Hakka, Gan, Xiang, Jin and others; wider Tibeto-Burman comparisons | Modeled family dates vary; neither Shang writing nor the family root dates Mandarin directly |
| Yoruba | Niger-Congo expansion estimate near 12,000 BP; local Proto-Yoruboid model 2500–300 BCE | Itsekiri, Igala and Yoruba varieties | Macrofamily expansion is broad and classification-sensitive; local range is model-specific |
| Hebrew | Canaanite evidence c. 1550–1200 BCE; Hebrew differentiates gradually near Late Bronze / Early Iron | Phoenician, Ammonite, Moabite, Edomite; Aramaic close but distinct | Gradual boundary, not a single founding date |

Age refers to an ancestor or an attested historical stage, not an unchanged modern language. Reconstructed stages are hypotheses. Inscriptions, archaeology-calibrated phylogenies and manuscript dates are different kinds of evidence. Unicode encodes characters and marks, not pen strokes or stroke order. These standard orthographies do not have native subscript letters; underdots and vowel points are diacritics.
