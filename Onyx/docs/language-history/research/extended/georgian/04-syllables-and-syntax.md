## Syllables, prosody, and syntax

Georgian syllables permit substantial consonant clusters, especially across morpheme boundaries and in verb forms. Stress is generally weak and not the main grammatical signal; pitch and intonation require dialect-aware description. Constituent order is flexible, with case marking, agreement, and information structure shaping interpretation; a simple SVO label is inadequate for the full system.

The descriptive labels here are broad summaries, not templates for every dialect or genre. Compare attested forms by date and register; syntax may change while a writing convention remains conservative. Phonological syllables, script units, aksharas, and Unicode grapheme clusters are distinct analytical units.
