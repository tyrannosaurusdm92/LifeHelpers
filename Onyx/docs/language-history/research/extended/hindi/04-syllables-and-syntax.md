## Syllables, prosody, and syntax

Hindi commonly uses SOV order, postpositions, split ergativity in perfective transitive clauses, and agreement patterns conditioned by case marking and construction. Syllables allow complex onsets in loans and limited codas; vowel length and nasalization contrast. Prosodic phrasing and stress are not written with routine accent marks in standard Devanagari.

The descriptive labels here are broad summaries, not templates for every dialect or genre. Compare attested forms by date and register; syntax may change while a writing convention remains conservative. Phonological syllables, script units, aksharas, and Unicode grapheme clusters are distinct analytical units.
