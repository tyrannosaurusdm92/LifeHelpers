## Sound history

POc phonology is reconstructed from cognates, sound correspondences, and subgroup comparisons in the Lexicon of Proto Oceanic. Reconstructed consonants and vowels use starred notation (for example *patu in a cited lexical set); descendant reflexes differ by branch and environment. A correspondence such as POc *p > Fijian v in one cognate set is not a universal sound change for every word.

For reliable sound-change claims, identify the source stage, daughter branch, conditioning environment, and counterexamples. Orthographic similarity alone cannot establish cognacy or sound correspondence. Reconstructed segments use an asterisk and remain hypotheses.
