## Syllables, prosody, and syntax

Reconstructed POc has open syllable tendencies and a rich system of particles, possessive constructions, and verb phrase morphology; these are inferred from descendant languages and should be tagged as reconstructed. Oceanic languages differ substantially in word order, alignment, and phonotactics. Since POc has no direct corpus, claims about discourse, prosody, and exact sentence patterns carry more uncertainty than comparative phoneme correspondences.

The descriptive labels here are broad summaries, not templates for every dialect or genre. Compare attested forms by date and register; syntax may change while a writing convention remains conservative. Phonological syllables, script units, aksharas, and Unicode grapheme clusters are distinct analytical units.
