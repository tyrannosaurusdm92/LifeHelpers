## Family placement

Proto-Oceanic (POc) is a reconstructed ancestor of the Oceanic branch of Austronesian, itself a member of Malayo-Polynesian. POc is not directly written or spoken today, and its reconstructed forms are hypotheses supported by regular correspondences among daughter languages. Oceanic branches include western and eastern groups, with Central Pacific and Polynesian histories downstream; subgrouping remains an active research area.

**Proposed root:** Proto-Oceanic — 3000–3300 BP, using 1950 CE as the conventional BP origin. The qualifying point estimate is 3200 BP. Confidence: moderate as an archaeology-informed reconstruction; subgroup boundaries and dates vary. This date concerns the stated ancestor; it is not an age claim for the modern language. 

**Siblings and comparisons:**
- Western Oceanic descendants (e.g., North New Guinea, Papuan Tip, Meso-Melanesian groups)
- Admiralty Islands languages
- Temotu languages (subgrouping debated)
- Central Pacific languages (including Fijian and Polynesian)
- Micronesian languages

Do not flatten distant relatives and immediate sister groups into a single branching level.
