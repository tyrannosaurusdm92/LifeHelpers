## Chronology and evidence

The comparative and archaeological literature places the POc community in the Bismarck Archipelago in the late second millennium BCE, often in connection with Lapita dispersal. The date is a model and not a dated written document. Subsequent dispersals produced numerous Oceanic branches across Near and Remote Oceania; dates for individual splits differ by method and evidence.

**Dating statement:** Comparative reconstruction combined with Lapita-related archaeological chronology; reconstructed speech community is usually placed in the Bismarck Archipelago in the late second millennium BCE.

Keep three clocks separate: (1) reconstructed ancestor age, (2) earliest surviving written attestation, and (3) emergence of a standardized modern variety. They answer different questions. A lack of early writing is not evidence that a language did not exist.
