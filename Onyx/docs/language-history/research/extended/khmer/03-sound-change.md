## Sound history

Modern Khmer has a complex vowel system and register contrasts linked historically to changes in the voicing of stops and the loss/reorganization of phonation contrasts. The chronology and phonetic details of register development remain debated. Khmer spelling is morphologically and historically conservative in places, so a grapheme sequence should not be treated as a narrow phonetic transcript.

For reliable sound-change claims, identify the source stage, daughter branch, conditioning environment, and counterexamples. Orthographic similarity alone cannot establish cognacy or sound correspondence. Reconstructed segments use an asterisk and remain hypotheses.
