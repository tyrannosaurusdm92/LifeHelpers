## Syllables, prosody, and syntax

Khmer has predominantly analytic morphology, uses particles and word order for grammatical relations, and generally follows SVO order in neutral clauses. Syllables can have complex onsets, a restricted set of codas, and register-sensitive vowel realizations. Khmer orthographic syllables and Unicode grapheme clusters do not always map one-to-one to spoken syllables.

The descriptive labels here are broad summaries, not templates for every dialect or genre. Compare attested forms by date and register; syntax may change while a writing convention remains conservative. Phonological syllables, script units, aksharas, and Unicode grapheme clusters are distinct analytical units.
