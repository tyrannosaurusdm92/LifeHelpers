## Family placement

Khmer belongs to Austroasiatic, specifically the Khmeric grouping in a classification whose internal subgrouping continues to be refined. Khmer is not a Tai language, despite long contact with Thai and Lao. Distant Austroasiatic comparisons such as Mon, Vietnamese, and Khasi do not imply immediate sisterhood; use branch-qualified trees.

**Proposed root:** Proto-Austroasiatic — 3000–4500 BP, using 1950 CE as the conventional BP origin. The qualifying point estimate is 3500 BP. Confidence: contested model; low confidence in exact chronology and homeland. This date concerns the stated ancestor; it is not an age claim for the modern language. 

**Siblings and comparisons:**
- Vietnamese (Vietic branch; distant Austroasiatic relative)
- Mon (Monic branch; distant relative)
- Khasi (Khasic branch; distant relative)
- Pearic languages (neighboring Austroasiatic group; not Khmer dialects)

Do not flatten distant relatives and immediate sister groups into a single branching level.
