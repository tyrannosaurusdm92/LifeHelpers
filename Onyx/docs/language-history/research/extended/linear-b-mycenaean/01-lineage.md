## Family placement

Linear B is a writing system; the language it records is Mycenaean Greek, the earliest directly written Greek dialect. Greek belongs to Hellenic within Indo-European. Mycenaean is compared with later Greek varieties including Arcado-Cypriot, Attic-Ionic, Aeolic, and Doric groups. Linear A is a different, undeciphered script tradition and should not be called Greek merely because Linear B adapted some signs from it.

**Proposed root:** Proto-Greek / early Greek dialect continuum — 3400–4000 BP, using 1950 CE as the conventional BP origin. The qualifying point estimate is 3600 BP. Confidence: moderate for chronology of Greek presence and linguistic identification; exact Proto-Greek split date is modeled. This date concerns the stated ancestor; it is not an age claim for the modern language. 

**Siblings and comparisons:**
- Attic-Ionic Greek (later branch/dialect group)
- Aeolic Greek
- Doric and Northwest Greek
- Arcado-Cypriot Greek (closest comparison group in many classifications)
Linear A belongs in the related-script comparison, not in the Greek language sibling list.

Do not flatten distant relatives and immediate sister groups into a single branching level.
