## Sound history

Linear B is an open-syllable logo-syllabary with signs broadly representing V, CV, and selected clusters. It poorly represents Greek consonant codas and several contrasts; scribes omit final consonants or use conventional spellings with “dummy” vowels. For example ti-ri-po can represent tripos. Sign values, spelling conventions, and dialect phonology must be kept analytically separate.

For reliable sound-change claims, identify the source stage, daughter branch, conditioning environment, and counterexamples. Orthographic similarity alone cannot establish cognacy or sound correspondence. Reconstructed segments use an asterisk and remain hypotheses.
