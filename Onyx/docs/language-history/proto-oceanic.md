# Proto-Oceanic

**Family:** Austronesian / Oceanic. **Root and evidence:** Proto-Oceanic, reconstructed around 3,000–3,300 BP; not directly attested and has no native script.

Detailed lineage, chronology, sound-change, syllable/syntax, and script/Unicode chapters are collected in the [extended atlas](research/extended/proto-oceanic/README.md). The age assessment distinguishes reconstructed roots from first attestations and records confidence and source keys.
