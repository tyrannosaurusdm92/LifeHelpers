# Dossier Canon Target — Onyx

## Identity

- Species: domestic cat (`Felis catus`).
- Sex: male.
- Breed influence: Domestic Shorthair / Maine Coon mix.
- Approximate weight: **27 lb**.
- Overall read: **very large, lengthy, substantial, sleek rather than fluffy**.
- Ordinary animation: natural four-legged feline anatomy.
- Professor is the only upright/human-like animation family; even then he remains cat-sized, unclothed, and may hold only a clipboard.

## Desired body read

### Forequarters

- Wide, stocky shoulders.
- Broad-ish muscular chest.
- Visible mass and shoulder travel while walking/flopping.
- Heavy but agile rather than slow, obese, or blocky.

### Torso

- Long body.
- Sleek short-to-medium coat must not be mistaken for a thin body.
- Do not pinch the waist merely to preserve “sleekness.”
- Body mass and coat fluff are separate concepts.

### Primordial pouch — mandatory identity detail

The dossier is unusually explicit here: the pouch is **low-hanging and visibly dynamic**. It should:

- compress,
- swing,
- lag behind body motion,
- bounce,
- settle after motion.

It is not a globally enlarged stomach and should not disappear because a waist-slimming pass makes the torso look leaner. The ribcage can remain controlled while the lower abdominal tissue gets secondary motion.

### Legs, paws, tail

- Long legs.
- Large feet.
- Relatively small reddish-black paw pads/toe beans.
- Long, relatively thin tail with strong taper and natural follow-through.

## Desired face

### Eyes

- Green-gold / green-yellow.
- Clearly visible in ordinary lighting.
- Never swallowed by shadow.
- Never visibly sunken deep into the skull.
- Catchlight/emissive support is acceptable only to preserve eye readability, not to make the eyes glow like lamps.

### Nose

- Clearly sculpted charcoal-to-black feline nose.
- Readable bridge.
- Readable nostril form.
- Must feel integrated into the muzzle rather than like a flat decal or floating patch.

### Mouth / muzzle

- Readable feline muzzle planes.
- Real whisker-pad volume.
- Mouth should not collapse into a flat seam on the black face.
- Chin/lower muzzle should remain legible under realistic lighting.

### Whiskers

- Mostly white.
- Silver/smoky-gray variation is allowed.
- Naturally curved and individually varied.
- Do not use rigid, perfectly mirrored, straight rods.

## Desired coat/material read

The first visual read must be **black cat**.

- Base: predominantly true black.
- Hair length: short to medium.
- Low fluff; no generic Maine Coon ruff/mane inflation.
- Charcoal highlights reveal form only as a lighting/material response.
- He must not read as a generally dark-gray cat.
- He must never read as a gray-and-white tuxedo.
- Sparse senior white/silver speckling is allowed and should remain subtle.
- Chest/belly may be a little fuller/grizzled while still reading black overall.
- Intended future material semantics include fur albedo, normal/roughness detail, senior speckle, nose/inner-ear/paw-pad details, and readable green-gold iris/catchlight behavior.

## Motion target

The dossier's animation library includes 26 named families across four groups:

### Core locomotion/posture

Idle/breathing, walk, leash walk, sit/stay/come, climb/jump, loaf, stretch.

### Rest/affection

Sleep/wake, snuggle, knead, slow blink, pet response.

### Voice/expression/support

Meow/yowl, purr, thinking/thoughtful, listening/caring, judgmental/refusal, service alert, professor.

### Object/room interaction

Eat/drink, groom/scratch, play/investigate, furniture entry/exit, purchase/layout reactions, clothing preview, tablet bird watching.

The 20 dossier pose images are expression/turnaround references. They are not a requirement to reproduce any anatomically odd or too-slender frame literally.

## Needs-free rule

Onyx has no draining hunger, thirst, health, energy, affection, comfort, attention, safety, or happiness meter. “Hungry” and “sleepy” are context-sensitive expressions only. He cannot die, become critically ill, run away, lose possessions, or punish inactivity.

## Locked identity

Final users may add removable clothing/accessories, but the game must not expose editing/replacement of Onyx's body, size, weight, proportions, build, face, fur, eyes, whiskers, tail, age detail, personality, relationships, service history, or psychiatric-support identity.
