# Third-party notices / research inspiration

This build was informed by user-supplied source repositories and research material.

## image2scan
The attached `image2scan` repository is Apache License 2.0. This build adapts general scanning concepts such as Gaussian blur, Canny-style edge extraction, contour approximation, and perspective-boundary reasoning. A copy of the repository license is included at `docs/third_party/IMAGE2SCAN_LICENSE.txt`.

## xyzalign-web
The attached `xyzalign-web` repository is BSD 3-Clause licensed. This build adapts the general idea of normalized directional/vector alignment when scoring room edge directions; it does not bundle the 3D molecular viewer. A copy of its license is included at `docs/third_party/XYZALIGN_LICENSE.txt`.

## SceneConductor
The supplied paper `SceneConductor: 3D Scene Generation from Single Image with Multi-Agent Orchestration` informed the mask cleanup, environment-scaffold, and localized-refinement organization. This browser scanner does not bundle or invoke the paper's external foundation models, point-map model, SAM3D pipeline, or Blender tooling.

## Fantasy map scanner
The user's older fantasy scanner supplied interaction concepts for trainable color samples, palette/hybrid classification, and click-to-sample training. Those ideas were rewritten around the living-room material taxonomy and do not retain fantasy-map geography features.
