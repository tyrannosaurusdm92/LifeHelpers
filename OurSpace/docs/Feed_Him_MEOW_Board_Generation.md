# Recipe board generation

The 50 authored board definitions remain the source of topology, linked boards, markers and special effects. They are **types**. `meow-board-generator.js` creates a fresh instance for every Market or Garden attempt and does not edit the source definitions.

Each recipe ID has a decimal sequence counter under `meow.retro.board-sequence.v1.<recipe ID>` in browser local storage. Market and Garden share it. The counter selects every type once per 50 visits, then returns to the types with new terrain and opening ingredients. Retry also consumes a new number. The board number is visible during play. A resumed puzzle starts a new attempt in the saved ingredient batch; Bon resumes its kitchen snapshot.

The generator keeps a fixed six-cell opening pocket for each recipe and type. It rearranges ordinary blockers and hazards away from that pocket, retaining their kinds, layers, counts, linked effects and reserved marker cells. A 3 × 2 or 2 × 3 pocket guarantees an initial three-piece Market trace and an adjacent Garden swap that makes a match. The remaining cells encode the visit number in base three using two nonoverlapping ingredient color groups on alternating cells. This avoids initial automatic matches. A recipe-specific reversible affine permutation changes many cells per visit without repeating the encoded opening while the counter fits the grid. The smallest type has 35 encoded cells, so each of the 50 types has at least `3^35` distinct encoded openings per recipe. The serial uses a decimal BigInt rather than a 32-bit random seed.

This is a practical collision-free range of more than 2.5 quintillion board visits per recipe within one retained browser profile. A finite mobile grid cannot have literally infinite distinct tile arrangements. The displayed decimal board number continues increasing after that range, but the tile encoding would eventually wrap. Clearing site storage also clears its history; a private session without storage only remembers boards until the tab closes. Simultaneous tabs do not share an atomic reservation lock.

All 5,001 recipe records now qualify for generation. A large order is split into Market batches of up to four fresh ingredients and Garden batches of up to six pantry ingredients. Each batch gets its own generated board. The ingredient ledger accumulates across the boards and carries every required ID to Bon Appétit; seafood is routed to the fresh Market phase, outside the pantry board.
