0. PURPOSE / PRECEDENCE
================================================================================

This file is the current gameplay dossier for Feed him MEOW.

- the old Pinball Fishing identity;

- the previous simplified Board 3 that ended after YUM/YUCK sorting and a mostly

-> RECIPE
-> MARKET MADNESS
-> INGREDIENT GARDEN
-> BON APPÉTIT
-> ONYX VERDICT
-> REWARD / NEXT LEVEL

- required ingredients;
- wrong-recipe ingredients;
- allowed substitutions;
- seasoning/sauce targets;
- cookware routing;
- final scoring;
- final Onyx judgment.

2. CURRENT HIGH-LEVEL FANTASY
================================================================================

A recipe appears.

The player first goes through MARKET MADNESS to collect the fresh/raw foundation
of the recipe.

The player then goes through INGREDIENT GARDEN to collect the pantry/liquid/dry
components.

Everything collected, plus recipe-plausible decoys and humorous trash, reaches
BON APPÉTIT.

BON APPÉTIT turns the final stage into a fast, physical-feeling assembly line:

1. Rotate the joystick to guide falling ingredients through the sorter.
2. Send correct recipe ingredients to YUM.
3. Send wrong-recipe ingredients and trash to YUCK.
4. Route accepted ingredients into the correct cooking vessel:
   - frying pan;
   - mixing bowl;
   - pot.
5. Send the vessels/components onward to the stove.
6. Keep the stove hot by continuously rotating/twirling the joystick.
7. DO NOT use a flipping mechanic.
8. When the recipe enters the oven step, blow to drive/maintain the oven action.
9. Use the shooter to hit the correct sauces and seasonings.
10. Avoid shooting ingredients/flavors the recipe does not call for.
11. The finished dish is revealed.
12. Onyx judges the whole level.

Onyx arrives/appears HUNGRY during Bon Appétit anticipation.

Final Onyx mood is determined by the final star result:

3, 4, or 5 stars
-> PURRING

0, 1, or 2 stars
-> JUDGMENTAL

This threshold is authoritative.

6. MARKET MADNESS — IDENTITY
================================================================================

MARKET MADNESS should read as a fast ingredient-connection puzzle.

If someone watches ten seconds of Market Madness, the first comparison should be:

"Pudding Pop-style line connecting with meat and produce."

Not:
"Pinball Fishing."

Not:
"Candy Crush with a market skin."

The PRIMARY PLAYER VERB is tracing/connecting.

Core loop:

1. Identify a connected path of matching ingredient tokens.
2. Press/touch/select the first token.
3. Drag through matching adjacent tokens.
4. Allow orthogonal connections.
5. Allow diagonal connections when enabled by the layout.
6. Do not reuse one token twice inside the same trace.
7. Release to resolve the chain.
8. Connected ingredients clear/collect.
9. Board refills/repositions.
10. Longer chains create stronger rewards/specials.
11. Continue until the market ingredient goals are satisfied or the move/resource
    budget is exhausted.

The interaction should feel deliberate and "snaky."

A player should frequently choose between:
- taking a short legal chain now;
- tracing a longer route to create a stronger special;
- routing through a special ingredient token;
- preserving a cluster for a larger later connection.

special generation candidate.

stronger special / bonus candidate.

high-value chain reward / dual special / large objective bonus candidate.

Exact values can be data-driven by difficulty.

22. BON APPÉTIT STAGE B — COOKWARE ROUTING
================================================================================

After an ingredient passes the YUM gate, it does not immediately disappear into
an oven.

It must be routed into the correct recipe vessel.

Primary vessel families:

FRYING PAN
For ingredients/components requiring pan/frying/sauté-style cooking.

MIXING BOWL
For ingredients/components that should be combined/mixed before heat or final
transfer.

POT
For ingredients/components requiring boiling, simmering, broth, pasta, rice,
soup, gravy, or similar pot-based handling.

The recipe specifies cookware routing.

Example:

BARBECUE CHICKEN
- chicken -> frying pan or recipe-defined roasting path;
- sauce components -> mixing bowl / flavor stage;
- broth component, if present -> pot only if recipe actually calls for it.

PASTA
- pasta + water -> pot;
- sauce ingredients -> pan/pot or bowl according to recipe definition.

BATTER
- flour + liquid + eggs -> mixing bowl;
- later moves onward to the recipe's heat path.

Do not route every YUM ingredient to the same vessel.

24. BON APPÉTIT STAGE C — STOVE HEAT
================================================================================

After cookware receives its ingredients, the vessels travel to the stove.

The stove interaction is NOT a flipping game.

NO FLIPPING.

The defining interaction is:
KEEP IT HOT BY ROTATING / TWIRLING THE JOYSTICK.

The player continuously rotates the joystick in circular motions.

This is intentionally inspired by the physical pumping/spinning energy of the
candy-kart assembly sequence.

The system tracks:

heatInput
heatMomentum
heatTarget
heatLowThreshold
heatHighThreshold
timeInTargetZone
timeTooCold
timeTooHot

The goal is not maximum heat.

The goal is to keep the recipe inside its target heat band.

Too little rotation:
- heat drops;
- cooking slows;
- texture/doneness score drops.

Correct sustained rotation:
- heat remains in the recipe's good band;
- cooking progress rises.

Wild/excessive sustained rotation:
- can push heat too high;
- burn/scorch risk rises.

26. BON APPÉTIT STAGE D — OVEN BLOW
================================================================================

When the active recipe has an oven/bake/roast step, the machine transfers the
relevant vessel/component into the oven.

The primary fantasy is:
THE PLAYER BLOWS TO DRIVE / MAINTAIN THE OVEN.

Preferred implementation:
- microphone-supported breath/blow detection when permission and hardware permit.

The player blows toward the microphone.

The system should detect sustained breath energy rather than speech content.

Track:
blowStrength
blowDuration
ovenHeat
ovenTarget
ovenProgress

The action should evoke feeding air/energy into the oven.

Do not require spoken words.

================================================================================
27. OVEN BLOW — ACCESSIBILITY / FALLBACK
================================================================================

Blowing cannot be the only possible input because:
- microphones may be unavailable;
- microphone permission may be denied;
- some players cannot safely/comfortably blow;
- noisy rooms can interfere with detection.

Therefore the same action must have an equivalent accessible fallback such as:

R / MAIN ACTION HOLD
- hold to blow;

or

RIGHT TRIGGER / LARGE TOUCH BUTTON
- hold/pulse to blow.

The game may visually show the same air/oven effect.

Using the fallback does not reduce rewards.

No information may be audio-only.

40. DIFFICULTY
================================================================================

MARKET MADNESS difficulty can grow through:
- longer required chains;
- irregular market layouts;
- more blockers;
- more objective types;
- more path choices;
- more decoy/filler types;
- tighter move budgets without becoming punitive.

INGREDIENT GARDEN difficulty can grow through:
- board geometry;
- blocker layering;
- ingredient quotas;
- portals;
- conveyors;
- drop routes;
- cascading dependencies;
- special-combination planning.

BON APPÉTIT difficulty can grow through:
- faster but still readable falling ingredients;
- more edible wrong-recipe decoys;
- more cookware routing distinctions;
- narrower but fair heat bands;
- longer heat sequences;
- more sauce/seasoning targets;
- more tempting wrong flavor targets;
- multi-component recipes.

Difficulty must NOT become:
- tiny unreadable targets;
- frame-perfect timing;
- mandatory microphone;
- mandatory multitouch;
- color-only identification;
- physically exhausting continuous input.

other fresh/raw ingredients. INGREDIENT GARDEN keeps its name and becomes the

seasonings and the other pantry/liquid/dry ingredients Market Madness does not
handle. BON APPÉTIT keeps and solidifies its name but completely changes its
gameplay: Onyx appears Hungry, then the player operates a colorful
Wreck-It-Ralph-candy-kart-creation-inspired cooking machine. The player rotates
the joystick to sort falling objects into YUM or YUCK, sending old boots, trash
and recipe-inappropriate ingredients to YUCK; routes accepted ingredients into
the correct frying pan, mixing bowl or pot; sends them to the stove; keeps the
heat up by twirling the joystick with NO flipping mechanic; blows to power the
oven when required; then aims and shoots the correct sauces and seasonings while
avoiding wrong flavors. The machine reveals the finished dish and the whole run
receives 0-5 stars. Onyx's final mood is fixed by that rating: 3 or more stars
means Purring, while fewer than 3 stars means Judgmental.
