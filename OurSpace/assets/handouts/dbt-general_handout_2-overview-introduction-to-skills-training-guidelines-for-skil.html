OurSpace shared-use support notes

Use this as a no-blame map. The point is not who failed; the point is what barrier showed up and what support would make the next try smaller.

Tiny steps

  • Name the moment without blame.
  • Circle one link: body, brain, access, trauma cue, timing, or environment.
  • Choose one repair or prevention step.
  • Save the lesson; do not punish the person.

Flare-day versions

  • Minimum: One breath, one label, one safe message, or one checkbox.
  • Medium: Two to five minutes with everything reachable from bed/chair.
  • Full: Use the original worksheet/handout when both bodies and brains have capacity.

Accessibility modifications

  • Do chain analysis verbally, by voice note, or with one checkbox.
  • Include pain, oxygen, MCAS, fatigue, dissociation, caregiver load, and mobility access as real links.

Relationship and caregiver notes

  • William's needs and Jasper's capacity both count.
  • Ask before helping; ask before escalating; name the smallest next step.
  • Pause means pause, not abandonment. I am coming back. I care about us. I need a smaller next step.
  • What is needed, what capacity exists, what can be smaller, and what can wait?

Avoid or adjust

  • Do not use this as a courtroom, interrogation, or proof that someone did not try.
