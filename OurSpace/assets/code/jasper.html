Context-Sensitive R / Y / X
R / Left ClickPrimary physical action with the active context: Hunt launch/paw target; knife cut; spoon stir; scoop; pour; seasoning; flip; mix; plating adjustment; contextual primary action.
Y / FInteract with or grab what the player is looking at: ingredient, tool, station, container, timer, Hunt ingredient or other contextual object.
X / Right ClickInspect or perform secondary manipulation: inspect ingredients/hazards/measurement/doneness/heat/dish/Onyx reaction, or rotate eligible objects.
Onyx in Kitchen Mode
Onyx receives no movement controls. He may watch, sniff, turn his head, look toward the active cook/pan, narrow his eyes, tail flick, grumble, paw, enter Thinking/Thoughtful/Judgmental/Hungry/Purring, and settle back down. Kitchen mistakes affect his reactions—not who the player controls.
Household Duo / Solo Kitchen
Household DuoBoth players use the same first-person Kitchen language and may work at separate eligible stations. Tasks may be swapped freely. No recipe requires both players, and one inactive player must not automatically fail the other.
SoloUses the same mechanics. Recipe/control flow must allow measure → cut → add → stir → heat → assemble → plate without simultaneous inaccessible actions.

Control Philosophy
HUNT: You are Onyx. WASD/Left Joystick moves him; Mouse/Right Joystick handles camera or Paw-Aim; Space/B jumps; C/A ducks; Shift/Z dashes; F/Y grabs.
KITCHEN: You are the human cooking for Onyx. WASD/Left Joystick moves; Mouse/Right Joystick looks/aims/manipulates; F/Y grabs; Left Click/R performs the physical action; Shift/Z is precision; Enter/B confirms/places; Esc/A cancels/sets down.

Control Ownership

Hunt: direct control · per-personality autonomy available

Hunt / Onyx Information

Button 3 controls Onyx status and autonomy. Autonomy is saved independently for each mood/personality; any manual movement or action temporarily overrides autonomous decisions without turning that personality's toggle off.

Kitchen / Onyx Supervisor Status

Button 3 is the Kitchen / Onyx supervisor status menu. The game runtime may supply current behavior, complaint intensity, ingredient/measurement/heat status, timers, preparation stage, recent correctable mistake and Household Duo teamwork state. It should not reveal hidden recipe answers unless a hint/accessibility feature permits it.
