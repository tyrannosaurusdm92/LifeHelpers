Distress Tolerance Handout 19 (Distress Tolerance Worksheet 16)

Community Reinforcement

Community reinforcement means replacing addiction reinforcers with abstinence reinforcers.

Reinforcing Abstinence Is Critical

Reinforcers in your environment play a powerful role in encouraging or discouraging addictive behaviors.

To stop addictive behavior, you have to figure out how to make a lifestyle without your addictive behaviors more rewarding than a lifestyle with your addictive behaviors.

You have to find a way to get behaviors incompatible with addictions to pay off and be rewarded by those around you.

Willpower is not sufficient. If it were, we would all be perfect!

Replace Addiction Reinforcers with Abstinence Reinforcers

Begin a series of action steps that will increase your chances of accumulating positive events to replace addictive behavior.
