Phase-specific inputs

Market Madness

Press, drag through matching connected market ingredients, release to collect. Keyboard/gamepad can navigate cells and confirm traces when the board implements them.

Ingredient Garden

Swap adjacent pantry/liquid ingredient pieces to form matches. Keyboard/gamepad navigate cells and select legal swaps; no mandatory precision gestures.

Bon Appétit

Twirl joystick for sorting and stove heat (not flipping). Hold R to blow without microphone. Aim with right stick/mouse/touch, R shoots; Z precision; X inspect; A cancels.

Remapping

Remapping updates shell key routing and informs the game adapter.
