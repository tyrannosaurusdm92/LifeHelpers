    Bulletin

    ✂ Bulletin Craft Studio

    Make stickers, glitter stencils, ink stamps and scrapbook accents. All editing stays in your browser.

    This is a local sticker maker, not an automatic AI subject extractor. For a photo background, tap the preview to remove adjoining pixels of a similar color.

    Live sticker preview

    Transparent pixels show as a checkerboard. Stickers become movable, independent items on the cork board.

    Choose a Post-it and stencil. The glitter is clipped to the stencil's exact shape, then bonded to that Post-it, moving with the note.

    Place stencil on Post-it

    Drag the glitter image inside this Post-it preview to choose the position. Press Apply to leave just the glitter-filled shape behind.

    Rubber-stamp a clover, botanical flower or leaf drawing on a selected Post-it. Choose the ink color and distressed texture.

    Stamp placement

    Drag the stamp on the paper to move it before applying it.

    These graphics are taken from the existing CC BY-SA 4.0 OpenMoji set already bundled with Bulletin. Add them as movable paperless decorations on your cork board.

    Use Arrange mode on the cork board to move and resize added decorations. The original taped clover, paper-vase flower and pinned stencil previews remain available.
